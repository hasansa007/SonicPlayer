Hello Apple Review Team,

Thank you for your feedback regarding Guideline 2.5.4.

SonicPlayer is a dedicated audio player application designed to let users listen to their personal music collection, podcasts, and audiobooks. Persistent background audio is a core feature of the app, allowing users to continue listening to their content while multitasking in other apps or when their device is locked.

The "audio" value in the `UIBackgroundModes` key is essential for this functionality. Without it, playback would stop immediately when the user leaves the app, which would severely degrade the user experience for an audio player.

We have implemented this feature using `AVAudioSession` with the `.playback` category.

**To verify the background audio feature:**
1. Launch SonicPlayer.
2. **Import an Audio File (in-app):**
    *   Navigate to the **Library** tab.
    *   Tap the **ellipsis (...) circle icon** in the top-right corner of the navigation bar.
    *   Select **"Import Files"** from the menu.
    *   Choose an audio file (MP3, M4A, or WAV) from your device's files (e.g., from iCloud Drive, On My iPad/iPhone, etc.) to import it into SonicPlayer.
3. Once the file is imported, tap on it in the **Library** tab to start playback.
4. While the audio is playing, press the Home button (or swipe up) to send the app to the background.
5. **Observation:** The audio continues to play.
6. Lock the device screen.
7. **Observation:** The audio continues to play, and playback controls are available on the Lock Screen.

We respectfully request you to re-evaluate the app with these updated steps.
