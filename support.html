SonicPlayer

Offline Music & Audio Player

About

SonicPlayer is built with passion to improve the audio learning experience for students and learners worldwide. We believe that accessing educational content should be simple, efficient, and enjoyable.

Key Features

  • Variable Speed Control: Listen at speeds from 0.5× to 2.0×.
  • Offline First: Your files available anytime, without internet.
  • Background Playback: Listen with your screen locked.
  • Format Support: Plays MP3, M4A, and WAV files seamlessly.

Quick Start Guide

1. Add Audio Files

Transfer MP3, M4A, or WAV files to your device's Documents folder using iTunes, Finder, or other file transfer methods.

2. Browse Your Files

Go to the Files tab to see all your audio files. Tap on any file to start playing it.

3. Control Playback

Use the Player tab to play, pause, skip, and adjust speed. The player works even when your screen is locked!

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add audio files?
Connect your device to your computer and use iTunes or Finder to add files to the SonicPlayer Documents folder. Supported formats: MP3, M4A, WAV.
Can I use SonicPlayer offline?
Yes! SonicPlayer is designed to work completely offline. All your audio files are stored locally on your device.
How do I change the playback speed?
In the Player view, tap the speed button (1.0×) at the bottom to choose from 0.5× to 2.0× speed options.

Troubleshooting

Files not showing up?
• Make sure files are in MP3, M4A, or WAV format
• Check that files are in the Documents folder
• Try tapping the refresh button in the Files tab
Audio not playing?
• Check device volume and mute switch
• Ensure the file is not corrupted
• Try playing a different file to test
