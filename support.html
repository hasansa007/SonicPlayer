SonicPlayer

Offline Audio Player

About

SonicPlayer is a clean, offline-first audio player for your personal library. Import your audio files, browse by folders, search quickly, and enjoy distraction-free playback.

Key Features

  • Offline First: Your files stay on your device.
  • Background Playback: Listen with your screen locked.
  • Format Support: Plays MP3, M4A, and WAV files.
  • Search & Organization: Browse folders, sort, and search your library.
  • Recording Mode: Record audio and edit recordings.

Quick Start Guide

1. Add Audio Files

Open the Library tab, tap the + button, and choose Import to add MP3, M4A, or WAV audio from Files/iCloud/On My iPhone.

2. Browse or Search

Use Library to browse folders, and use search at the top to quickly find what you want.

3. Control Playback

Tap any file to play. Use play/pause, skip, and speed controls. Playback continues in the background and from the Lock Screen.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add audio files?
Go to Library → tap + → Import. Supported formats: MP3, M4A, WAV.
Can I use SonicPlayer offline?
Yes! SonicPlayer is designed to work completely offline. All your audio files are stored locally on your device.
How do I change the playback speed?
Open the player and tap the speed control to choose from 0.5× to 2.0×.
Does SonicPlayer record audio?
Yes. Use the mode button in the header to switch to Recording and edit recordings (trim or delete sections).

Troubleshooting

Files not showing up?
• Make sure files are in MP3, M4A, or WAV format
• Try importing again from Library → + → Import
• Try pulling to refresh in the Library view
Audio not playing?
• Check device volume and mute switch
• Ensure the file is not corrupted
• Try playing a different file to test
