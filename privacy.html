Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 22, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how SonicPlayer ("we", "us", or "our") handles your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Data Collection

We do not collect any personal data. SonicPlayer is designed to function completely offline. We do not track your usage, collect your audio files, or transmit any data to external servers.

2. Local Storage

Any audio files, settings, or preferences you save within the app are stored locally on your device. You have full control over these files and can delete them at any time through the app or your device's file manager.

3. Microphone Access (Recording Mode)

If you switch to Recording Mode, SonicPlayer will request microphone permission to record audio. Recordings are stored locally on your device. SonicPlayer does not upload recordings or microphone data to any servers.

4. Third-Party Services

The app does not use any third-party analytics or advertising services that collect user data.
